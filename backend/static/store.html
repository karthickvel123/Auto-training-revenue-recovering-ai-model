⚡ Autonomous Recovery Architecture
1. Webhook Ingestion & Deduplication:
When payment fails, Razorpay fires payment.failed. FastAPI verifies HMAC-SHA256 signature and deduplicates.
2. Real-time Failure Classification:
Categorizes error into temporary, customer_action, permanent, or security with <15ms fallback reasoning.
3. 7 Deterministic Fintech Safety Rules:
Hard-coded gates prevent spamming, verify high-value bounds, check max retries (3), and block fraud.
4. Adaptive Strategy & Closed Learning Loop:
Dispatches 1-click alternative recovery. Successful recovery reinforces strategy weights.
